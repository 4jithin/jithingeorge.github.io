Meet Your Provider

Shoji Aji, MSN, APRN

Nurse Practitioner · ANCC Board-Certified

With nearly three decades in healthcare, Shoji Aji brings a rare depth of experience to every home visit. Her career began at the bedside — 20 years as a critical care registered nurse, caring for patients through their most fragile moments. That foundation is exactly what you feel when she walks through your door: a calm, steady presence who has seen it all and knows what matters.

Over the past 9 years as a nurse practitioner, Shoji has cared for patients across nearly every setting — urgent care, primary care, skilled and long-term care, hospital care, and home health. That breadth means she sees the whole picture of your health, coordinates confidently with your other providers, and knows precisely when something needs a closer look.

She earned her Master of Science in Nursing from Nova Southeastern University and is board-certified through the ANCC. Today, she brings that expertise to where it makes the biggest difference — directly to your home.
